Practice · A notebook in your browser

Python playground

A mini-notebook: the cells below share variables (run them top to bottom, like Jupyter). Use it to experiment with any model from the site, rehearse a take-home, or test an idea during interview prep. Everything runs locally in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

🧰 What's available

NumPy, pandas, scikit-learn, SciPy, statsmodels, matplotlib, XGBoost and LightGBM load automatically when you import them. Built-in datasets: load_iris, load_wine, load_breast_cancer, load_diabetes, load_digits, plus generators like make_classification, make_regression, make_blobs, make_moons. Internet downloads (e.g. fetch_openml) don't work in-browser. Use plt.show() to display charts. If something hangs, press ⏹ Stop (this resets all variables).
